Critical Data Safety Guidelines

If your drive contains critical information, immediate actions dictate the probability of recovery.

DO:

  • Stop unnecessary use of the device immediately upon first symptom.
  • Disconnect the affected drive to prevent sector degradation.
  • Document the events leading directly up to the failure.
  • Seek a formal laboratory diagnosis prior to executing software scans.

DON'T:

  • Do not repeatedly power-cycle a clicking, grinding, or buzzing drive.
  • Do not format or initialize an unrecognized drive in Disk Management.
  • Do not run `chkdsk` or aggressive recovery software over failing hardware.
  • Do not disassemble drive screws outside of an ISO Class 5 clean bench.
